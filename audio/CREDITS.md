# Audio credits and license

Every file in this folder is original and synthesized in code by `tools/audio/`
(numpy/scipy, rendered with `python3 tools/audio/build.py`, MP3 encoded with LAME).
No samples, soundfonts, sample packs or AI generators were used.

They are part of Guide The Ball and are released under the same terms as the rest of
this repository's original assets. Re-run the generator to rebuild them byte-for-byte
from the seeds in `tools/audio/sfx.py` and `tools/audio/music.py`.
