# Guide The Ball

A small physics puzzle made with Godot 4.6: launch balls, rotate Energy Walls and guide every ball into the goal circle.

Play on itch.io: https://kuthorx.itch.io/guide-the-ball

## Controls

- `Space` - launch a ball
- Mouse wheel over an Energy Wall - rotate it
- Left mouse drag - move the camera
- `R` - replay

## Languages

The game supports **English** and **简体中文**.

- On first launch the language follows the OS / browser language (Chinese -> 中文, otherwise English).
- Click the `ZH` / `EN` button in the top-right corner to switch; the choice is saved to `user://settings.cfg`.
- All strings live in `i18n/translations.csv` (`keys,en,zh`) and are registered under `[internationalization]` in `project.godot`.
- All text (Latin and Chinese) uses one pixel font, `fonts/FusionPixel-12px-zh_hans-subset.ttf` (Fusion Pixel Font 12px monospaced zh_hans, SIL OFL 1.1; licenses in `fonts/`). It is subset to the characters used in `i18n/translations.csv`. After adding or changing any text, regenerate it from the upstream TTF (https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font):

  ```sh
  python3 tools/fonts/build_fonts.py --src <dir containing fusion-pixel-12px-monospaced-zh_hans.ttf>
  ```

## Art

All sprites are generated by code (Pillow): `python3 tools/art/gen_art.py` writes `images/art/`. Tracks, boiling zones and the frame are drawn live (`scripts/BubbleTrack.gd`, `shaders/boil.gdshader`, `scripts/Frame.gd`). The direction is documented in `docs/art-direction.md`.

## Audio

Music and SFX are composed programmatically by AI (Claude) and rendered with Vital / Serum 2 / MS Basic soundfont, plus numpy synthesis and pedalboard's built-in effects. `tools/audio/compose.py` writes the MIDI and render specs; `arch -arm64 /tmp/audiokit/venv/bin/python tools/audio/build.py` renders `audio/music/*.ogg` and `audio/sfx/*.ogg`/`*.wav` (Ogg Vorbis for music and cues over 0.5 s; needs the local audiokit toolchain and ffmpeg). The direction, cue sheet, presets and licence notes are in `docs/audio-direction.md`.

Audio credits: Vital by Matt Tytel (GPL-3.0) with presets from In The Mix, Level 8, Databroth and Mr Bill; Serum 2 (Xfer Records) factory presets; the MuseScore MS Basic soundfont (MIT licence).

In game, click `Sound` in the top-right corner to step the volume, or press `M` to mute.

## Export

Presets: `Windows Desktop` and `Web` (single-threaded, for itch.io).

```sh
godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --export-release "Web" ../guide-the-ball-export/web/index.html
```
